COLORS
Type these in by hand. There are only five, and you will get to know them.

  ink      #191715     text, the nav bar, the footer
  paper    #fbfaf0     the page behind everything
  bone     #e1d8c8     the ingredients panel, the hairlines between sections
  accent   #1987af     links, the button, the step numbers, the eyebrow
  muted    #7d766f     the lede, the card copy, the small print

Keep a comment at the top of styles.css with these five in it. That is where
you will look every time you need one.

Want your own palette instead? Swap all five. Keep one very dark, one very
light, one mid, and one accent that is not any of the others.
