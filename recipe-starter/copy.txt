-- hero ------------------------------------------------------------
eyebrow:  Dinner · serves 4 · 40 minutes
h1:       Sticky BBQ Chicken
lede:     One tray, one sauce, and a forty minute window where you do almost nothing. Start it before you set the table.
image:    ../images/bbq-chicken-banner.webp

-- ingredients  (an unordered list, bullets left on) ----------------
  8 chicken thighs, skin on
  1 tbsp olive oil
  2 tsp smoked paprika
  1 tsp garlic powder
  200 ml barbecue sauce
  2 tbsp honey
  1 tbsp cider vinegar
  Salt and black pepper
  Spring onions, to finish

-- jump list  (an ordered list, one link per step) ------------------
  Heat the oven
  Season the chicken
  Mix the sauce
  Roast, then glaze
  Rest and serve

-- the five steps --------------------------------------------------

id="step-1"
  number: 1
  h3:     Heat the oven
  copy:   220°C, fan on. Put the empty tray in while it heats. A hot tray is the difference between crisp skin and steamed skin.

id="step-2"
  number: 2
  h3:     Season the chicken
  copy:   Pat the thighs dry, then rub with oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Dry skin browns. Wet skin does not.

id="step-3"
  number: 3
  h3:     Mix the sauce
  copy:   Stir the barbecue sauce, honey and vinegar together. Taste it. It should be sweeter than you want, because the oven takes the edge off.

id="step-4"
  number: 4
  h3:     Roast, then glaze
  copy:   Roast skin side up for 25 minutes. Brush on half the sauce, roast 10 more. Brush on the rest, roast 5 more. Three coats, not one.

id="step-5"
  number: 5
  h3:     Rest and serve
  copy:   Five minutes on the tray before it moves. Scatter the spring onions. Serve with the macaroni salad if you made it yesterday.
