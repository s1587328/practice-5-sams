RECIPE SITE · STARTER ASSETS
GDES 245 · Week 05 · Practice Four

There is no code in here. That is on purpose: you type the code.
What is in here is everything that is tedious to invent while you are
learning a layout.

  images/        eight photos. Five are used in the example, three are
                 spares so your three cards do not have to match mine.
  copy.txt       every headline, paragraph, ingredient and step, laid out
                 in the order you will need them.
  colors.txt     the five colors, and what each one is for.
  fonts.txt      the two typefaces, the <link> tags, and the CSS.

HOW TO START

  1. Make the repository first. The exercise page walks you through it.
  2. Make three folders: css, images, recipes.
  3. Drag the contents of images/ above into your own images folder.
  4. Keep copy.txt open beside VS Code and work down it.

MAKE IT YOURS

Every one of these files is a default, not a requirement. Swap the photos
for your own, write your own recipe, pick two different typefaces, choose
five different colors. The grade is on the structure: the nav, the grid,
the lists, and the links that actually go where they say they go.

A note on the photos: they are here for coursework. Do not republish them
as your own.
